Common questions

Before you call

Do you take urgent or after-hours jobs?

We're on the job Monday–Saturday, 8am–5pm. For something urgent outside those hours, call or WhatsApp us — evening availability can often be arranged for pressing jobs and events.

How far ahead should I book for an event?

As early as you can, especially around fete season. We set up temporary sinks, water lines, and drainage ahead of the event — the earlier we know your date, the easier it is to lock in.

Do you cover my parish?

Yes — we travel island-wide, all 11 parishes from St. Lucy to Christ Church, for homes, job sites, and event venues alike.

How do I get a quote?

Call, WhatsApp, or send your details through the form below. Most jobs start with a site visit and a written estimate before any work begins.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash, bank transfer, or cheque.

Do you work with contractors and developers?

Yes — water main installation, underground pipe fitting, and pressure and leak testing for new housing developments, alongside our household and event work.
